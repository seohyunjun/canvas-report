anime.js 3.2.2 · MIT · 17 KB — the smallest of the three motion engines. Its signature is anime.stagger(), which distributes delay by time, by value, or by grid position. canvas-report bans that effect (references/motion.md: six cards rising in sequence delays reading six times).
